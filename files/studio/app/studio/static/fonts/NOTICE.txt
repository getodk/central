Roboto
======

RobotoMedium.woff2, RobotoBold.woff2) are Copyright Google Inc. and are
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:

    http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

They are bundled so that Central Studio matches the Survey Solutions interface
without depending on an external font service, which would not work on the
offline and low-connectivity servers Central is often deployed to.
